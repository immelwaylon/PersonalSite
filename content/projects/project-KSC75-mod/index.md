## Project Overview
This project involved creating a custom detachable cable system for Koss KSC75 headphones by designing a 3D-printed mounting solution and performing precise soldering work. The modification replaces the fragile fixed cable with a reliable 2-pin connector system, significantly improving the headphones' longevity and user experience.

## Design & Engineering Process

### Source Analysis and Selection
Evaluated existing modification approaches:
- **[Screw-mounted 2-pin mod](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6786806)** - Provided optimal connector type but required unavailable hardware
- **[Snap-in MMCX mod](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:5031517)** - Offered desired snap-fit installation but used less reliable MMCX connectors

### Design Integration
Combined the best aspects of both designs:
- **2-pin connector system** for superior mechanical reliability
- **Snap-fit mounting mechanism** for tool-free installation
- **Custom retention features** to prevent connector displacement under strain


### Critical Modifications
- **Added internal ridges** to securely lock the 2-pin connector in place
- **Optimized clearance** for soldering access and wire management
- **Ear clip clearance optomization** for comfortable wear positioning

## Implementation

### Manufacturing
- **3D printed** using PLA material for durability and dimensional accuracy
- **Precision soldering** of miniature 2-pin connector pads
- **Secure assembly** using epoxy adhesive for mechanical stability

### Technical Challenges
- **Micro-soldering** on extremely small connector pads (≤2mm)
- **Connector retention** without mechanical fasteners
- **Maintaining original headphone aesthetics** and ergonomics
- **Ensuring reliable electrical connections** despite mechanical stress

## Final Result
The [completed design](https://www.tinkercad.com/things/eUHoCDDXJ5D-snap-in-2-pin-ksc-75-mod) provides:
- **Secure snap-fit installation** without screws or special tools
- **Standard 2-pin connector compatibility** with aftermarket cables
- **Full pivot functionality** maintained for comfort
